# Skill: Problem Solving
Handling blockers and complex logic.

### 1. EVALUATE
- **Payload**: `{"action": "EVALUATE", "text": "reasoning"}`
- **Usage**: Use this when you are stuck or need to pause and plan. Explain your reasoning in the `text` field. This does not perform a browser action but helps keep the mission on track.

### 2. COMPLETE
- **Payload**: `{"action": "COMPLETE", "text": "summary"}`
- **Usage**: Finalize the mission and provide a summary of what was accomplished.
